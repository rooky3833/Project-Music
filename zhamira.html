Zhamira

Zhamira (full name Zhamira Zambrano) is a Venezuelan singer-songwriter based in Puerto Rico, known for Latin pop. Her debut studio album is Curita Para El Corazón, and she has collaborated with her husband, Jay Wheeler, on songs like “Extrañándote.”
