Rauw Alejandro

Rauw Alejandro is a Puerto Rican singer, songwriter, and producer from Capetillo, a neighborhood in San Juan. He is known for blending reggaeton, Latin trap, R&B, and electronic music, with hits like “Todo de Ti” and “Tattoo.”
