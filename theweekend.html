The Weeknd

The Weeknd (real name Abel Tesfaye) is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and producer from Toronto, Ontario. He is known for his dark R&B and pop sound and hits like “Blinding Lights” and “Starboy,” and he headlined the 2021 Super Bowl halftime show.
