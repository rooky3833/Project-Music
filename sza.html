SZA

SZA (real name Solána Rowe) is an American R&B singer-songwriter born in St. Louis, Missouri, and raised in Maplewood, New Jersey. She is known for her debut album Ctrl and hits like “Kill Bill” and “Snooze” from her album SOS.
