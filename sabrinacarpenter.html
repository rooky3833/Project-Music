Sabrina Carpenter

Sabrina Carpenter is an American singer-songwriter and actress from Quakertown, Pennsylvania. She began her career on the Disney Channel show Girl Meets World and rose to pop stardom with hits like “Espresso” and “Please Please Please” from her albums Short n’ Sweet and Man’s Best Friend.
