About Tunely

Tunely is a place to discover artists and explore their music. Browse artist profiles for short introductions, album artwork, and audio previews, all in one simple music discovery experience.

Explore at your own pace

Search for an artist by name on the home page, browse the full artist collection, or use “Surprise me” to jump to a random profile. Each profile brings together a short introduction, selected albums, and audio previews so you can get a feel for the artist and their sound.

A growing collection

Tunely is an evolving project, and the artists featured here are just a starting point. Have someone in mind? Use the suggestion form to share an artist, their genre or style, and why you think they deserve a feature.

Suggestions are saved only in your current browser. They are not sent to the site owner or automatically added to the artist collection.
